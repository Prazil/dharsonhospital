FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Patient Guidance & FAQs

Answers to common questions about preparing for your orthopaedic appointment and consultation.

You should consult an orthopaedic specialist if you experience persistent joint pain, swelling, difficulty bearing weight, reduced range of motion, severe stiffness after rest, numbness or tingling in the limbs, or immediately following an acute injury, fall, or trauma.
Please bring a valid photo identification, any previous medical records, past X-rays, MRI scans or laboratory reports, a list of current medications you are taking, and details of any previous surgeries or existing health conditions.
Yes, bringing past radiological imaging (X-rays, CT, MRI scans) and blood test reports is very helpful. It provides our doctors with a complete clinical history, prevents unnecessary repeat scans, and assists in accurate comparative evaluation.
An initial consultation typically takes between 20 to 40 minutes. This includes a thorough discussion of your symptoms and medical history, physical examination of the affected joint or bone, review of any diagnostic scans, and discussing recommended care pathways.
After completing your clinical examination and reviewing diagnostic findings, your orthopaedic doctor will explain your condition clearly. We will outline suitable treatment avenues ranging from conservative physical therapy and medication to minimally invasive procedures or surgical options, allowing you to make an informed decision.
Yes, follow-up consultations can be booked directly through our front desk, by calling the hospital, or by requesting an appointment online through this website. Regular follow-ups ensure your recovery is progressing smoothly.
